A few practical questions.

You do not need to arrive with a tool picked out or a finished plan.

Do we need to be an N45 managed IT customer?+

No. AI consulting, automation projects, and managed AI are available independently. We can work alongside the people who already support your technology.

How much does an AI project cost?+

It depends on the workflow, the systems involved, and the support needed. After the initial conversation, N45 can propose a defined paid pilot. Implementation, software or usage charges, and optional ongoing management are explained before you commit.

What if ordinary automation would do the job?+

We use the simplest approach that fits. A clear process or a straightforward connection between two systems may solve the problem. AI is useful when the task needs help interpreting information or preparing a draft.

How do you handle business information and mistakes?+

We agree on the information a workflow may use, review the chosen tools' data handling, and define access and approval rules. AI can make mistakes, so we test representative tasks, plan for exceptions, and keep the right decisions with your team.

What would you like your team to spend less time doing?

Bring one recurring task to a 30-minute conversation. We will help you work out a practical next step.
