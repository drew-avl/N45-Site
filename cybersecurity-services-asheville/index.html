A useful security review

Leave with priorities, not panic.

A practical assessment should tell leadership what matters first, what can wait, who owns each step, and how the business will know the work is complete.

01 · DISCOVER

Map the environment

Understand accounts, devices, cloud services, networks, vendors, data, backup paths, and existing controls.

02 · VERIFY

Check what is really enforced

Confirm configurations and operational reality instead of assuming a purchased license means a safeguard is active.

03 · PRIORITIZE

Rank by likelihood and impact

Separate urgent exposure from longer-term improvement and avoid spending effort where it changes little.

04 · DOCUMENT

Assign owners and evidence

Record the decision, next action, responsible party, and validation so improvements survive staff and vendor changes.

Risk looks different by operation

Security shaped around the business you actually run.

A healthcare practice, accounting firm, hotel, and contractor do not carry the same data, systems, deadlines, or disruption costs. N45 builds from a dependable baseline, then adapts controls to the operation and its obligations.

  • Healthcare practices: HIPAA-aware identity, device, vendor, access, backup, and technical safeguard documentation.
  • Legal and accounting firms: confidential file access, Microsoft 365 security, client workflow protection, and continuity during deadlines.
  • Hospitality businesses: guest and operations network separation, POS connectivity, account ownership, and recovery planning.
  • Manufacturers and trades: office and field identity, rugged endpoints, remote access, vendor connections, and production continuity.

No technology provider can promise a business will never experience an incident or guarantee regulatory compliance. N45’s role is to reduce preventable exposure, improve detection and recovery, document safeguards, and help leadership make informed decisions.

Cybersecurity questions

Start with what materially reduces risk.

What cybersecurity services does an Asheville small business need first?

Most businesses should begin with multifactor authentication, safer administrative access, supported and patched devices, endpoint protection, email and identity safeguards, tested backups, and a reliable employee offboarding process. The order should be based on the actual environment and risks.

Does N45 perform cybersecurity assessments?

Yes. An assessment reviews the current environment, access, devices, Microsoft 365 controls, backups, vendors, documentation, and other relevant safeguards. The useful output is a prioritized next-step plan, not a generic list of every security product available.

Can N45 help with HIPAA cybersecurity?

N45 can implement and document technical safeguards that support a healthcare organization’s HIPAA security program, including identity, device, access, backup, and vendor controls. Technology services do not by themselves guarantee legal compliance.

Can cybersecurity be included with managed IT support?

Yes. Security is most effective when it is part of daily identity, device, support, and change-management work. N45 can provide focused security projects or include ongoing controls within a documented managed IT scope.
