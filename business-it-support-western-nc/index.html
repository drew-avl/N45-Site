Where N45 works

Local context matters when the issue cannot be fixed from a screen.

Remote support is usually the fastest answer for an account, application, or user issue. Physical problems are different. A failing access point, misbehaving switch, office move, internet handoff, or device rollout benefits from someone who knows the region and can be on site.

  • Asheville and Leicester: local business IT support, Microsoft 365, networks, devices, and managed service.
  • Arden and the I-26 corridor: office, warehouse, professional practice, and multi-site connectivity support.
  • Hendersonville: responsive remote support plus planned or urgent on-site work when required.
  • Black Mountain and surrounding communities: support for independent businesses, hospitality, professional teams, and nonprofits.

For locations farther across Western North Carolina, N45 defines response expectations and on-site coverage before work begins so there is no ambiguity when a physical visit is needed.

Common support questions

Know what happens before you need help.

Does N45 offer both remote and on-site business IT support?

Yes. Remote support resolves many user and software problems quickly. N45 also provides on-site support across the Asheville area and Western North Carolina when hardware, cabling, networks, or facilities require hands-on work.

Can N45 support us without taking over everything?

Yes. Support can begin with a focused project, assessment, Microsoft 365 cleanup, network improvement, or a defined co-managed role when that is the right fit. Scope, ownership, and escalation are written down.

How does N45 reduce repeated support problems?

N45 tracks recurring issues back to their cause, documents the fix, establishes standards, and reviews support patterns instead of treating every repeat problem as a new isolated ticket.

Do you support home computers?

N45 is focused on the technology behind businesses and organizations. The service is designed around company users, systems, security, networks, vendors, and operating continuity rather than general residential computer repair.
