# N45 visual identity

## Brand idea

N45 should feel like Western North Carolina at its best: capable, independent, grounded, and quietly modern. The identity combines a precise 45-degree line with the steady profile of an `N`. The warm point is a sunrise and a directional marker—a small signal that N45 helps clients find the next practical step.

The regional signature is `Serving Western North Carolina`. Use it beneath the primary wordmark when space allows; omit it in compact placements rather than compressing or artificially stretching the text.

## Logo files

- `n45-mark.svg`: standalone mark for avatars, app icons, and compact placements
- `n45-lockup-dark.svg`: primary lockup for light and warm backgrounds
- `n45-lockup-light.svg`: reversed lockup for dark green or photographic backgrounds
- `favicon.svg`: simplified small-format mark
- `ridge-pattern.svg`: supporting topographic/ridgeline texture

Keep clear space around the mark equal to roughly one quarter of its width. Do not recolor the diagonal, stretch the mark, add shadows, or place the dark lockup on low-contrast backgrounds.

## Typography

- Display: Newsreader, with Georgia as fallback
- Interface and body: Manrope, with Segoe UI as fallback
- Utility labels: IBM Plex Mono, with Consolas as fallback

## Voice

Write like a trusted local operator: calm, direct, useful, and specific. Prefer practical outcomes over product names. Avoid fear-based security language, oversized claims, and technology jargon without a plain-English explanation.

Preferred themes: steady operations, local accountability, documented systems, clear next steps, and resilience.
